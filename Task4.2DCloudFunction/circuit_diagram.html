📦 Component List

  • Arduino Nano 33 IoT (1x)
  • PIR HC-SR501 Motion Sensor (1x)
  • BH1750 Digital Light Sensor (1x)
  • Slider Switch SPDT 3-pin (1x)
  • LED - Red for Porch Light (1x)
  • LED - Green for Hallway Light (1x)
  • 220Ω Resistor (2x)
  • Breadboard (1x)
  • Jumper Wires M-M and M-F (Multiple)
  • USB Cable for Arduino (1x)

⚠️ Important Notes

  • PIR sensor requires 5V for proper operation
  • BH1750 operates at 3.3V - do NOT connect to 5V!
  • Internal pull-up resistor enabled on D3 (Switch pin)
  • Both D2 and D3 are configured as hardware interrupt pins
  • I2C communication uses A4 (SDA) and A5 (SCL)
  • LED current-limiting resistors: 220Ω for both LEDs
  • All GND connections share common ground
  • PIR sensor needs 30 seconds stabilization after power-on
  • Interrupt modes: PIR = RISING edge, Switch = FALLING edge
